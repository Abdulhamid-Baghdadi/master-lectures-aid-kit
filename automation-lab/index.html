Advanced Automation

After the Rechenbeispiele of Kubinger (2021a), the lab tasks of Kubinger (2021b) and Kubinger and Lang (2021), and the slides of Kubinger (2021), Advanced Automation (AAU), FH Technikum Wien.

A learning aid: every curve worked out the same way on every visit, every MATLAB snippet written from the view's current values. Pages are PDF pages; no text of the course is reproduced. Where a solution sheet gives a value, the lab shows it beside its own and says when they differ.
