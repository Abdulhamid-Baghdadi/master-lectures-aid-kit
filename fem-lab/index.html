Finite Elements

After the slides of Krach (2018, 2020a, 2021), CAE – Finite Elemente, FH Technikum Wien, 2nd semester, and the solved ModSim tasks of Puhwein (2020).

A learning aid: linear elastic springs, bars and beams in N, mm and MPa, small dense solvers, every result worked out the same way on every visit. Pages are the PDF pages of each slide set; no text of the slides is reproduced. Where the slides only name a method and the lab computes it, the lab says so.
