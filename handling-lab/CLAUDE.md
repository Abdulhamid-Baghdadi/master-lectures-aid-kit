# Industrial Handling

Interactive labs for the FH Technikum Wien master's course Industrial Handling (IHA, Handhabungstechnik): one lab per
idea, from the mobility count to suction grippers. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Sources, in `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\FH\03_FH_Master\3. Semester\IHA\`: the lecture notes of Berger (2021),
chapters 1–6 as `IHA_KAP_*.pdf` (text layer; read with `pdftotext -enc UTF-8`; pages cited are PDF pages, Blatt where the
footer is readable), the Übungsskript and the solved Übungen 5_1–5_3 in `Übung\`. Unreadable (images or scans): the
VDI 2143 law tables (ch. 5, pp. 25–29), `Formelsammlung.docx`, the Kap. 4 solutions and `Kurventafel_SK_KS.pdf`; read
them from the page images before citing them. Known slips in the sources: Übung 5_3 uses ω = 5.54 s⁻¹ for 50 rpm
(should be 5.236 s⁻¹); ch. 3, p. 69 gives B in henry (should be tesla). Not in the course, so not here: the Geneva
wheel and vibratory-feeder equations. Check every equation against the notes and cite chapter and page. Part of
`../PLAN.md`; not published.
