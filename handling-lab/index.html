Industrial Handling

After the lecture notes of Berger (2021), Industrial Handling (IHA), FH Technikum Wien, and its Übungsskript with the solved Übungen 5_1 to 5_3.

A learning aid: ideal rigid links and joints without play or friction, every drawing worked out the same way on every visit. Pages are the PDF pages of each chapter's file; no text of the notes is reproduced. Where the course draws and this lab computes, the lab says so.
