Master lectures aid kit

Interactive labs for the master's programme at FH Technikum Wien, built from the lecture notes, slides and exercises. Open a lab; its "← Aid kit home" link brings you back here.

Please note: the material dates from 2021 onwards. Courses, their content and the curriculum may have changed since; check the current course pages. This is a personal learning aid, not official FH Technikum Wien material.

Industrial Handling Lab

Industrial Handling (IHA, Handhabungstechnik), after Berger (2021)

The mobility count, Grashof's rule, the transmission angle, instant centres, dead-centre and two-position synthesis, coupler curves, motion laws, the disc cam, the indexing cam, and suction and magnet grippers.

Robot Kinematics Lab

Industrieroboter (IRB), 1st semester

Degrees of freedom, frames and rotations, Euler and roll-pitch-yaw angles, the DH convention, the ABB IRB 120, Jacobians and singularities, inverse kinematics.

Mobile Robots and Vision Lab

Mobile und Service Robotik (MUS, MUS2), 2nd and 3rd semester

Bayes, Kalman, EKF and particle filters for localisation; denoising, thresholding, HSV, edges, a convolution layer, stereo depth and RANSAC.

Sensor Systems Lab

Advanced Sensor Systems (ASENS), 1st semester

Echo ranging, image data rates, the pinhole camera and its calibration, lens distortion, colour, time of flight and stereo depth.

Finite Elements Lab

CAE and FEM, 2nd semester

Springs and assembly, the bar and beam under mesh refinement, shape functions, Gauss points, a nonlinear spring by Newton, modes and time steps.

Advanced Automation Lab

Advanced Automation (AAU), 2nd semester

The course's exercises step by step, each step with the MATLAB that gives it: models, normal forms, controllability, pole placement, observers, linearisation, the motor and the crane trolley.

ModSim Lab

Modellbildung und Simulation (ModSim), 2nd semester

The lecture's worked examples step by step, each step with its MATLAB: free-body diagrams, Lagrange term by term, the Simulink integrator chain, state space, pendulums, a chaotic double pendulum, two-mass eigenmodes, an RLC circuit, a motor and a shaker.

Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. The only thing kept is your light or dark choice.
