# Mobile and Service Robotics

Interactive labs for the FH Technikum Wien master's courses Mobile- und Servicerobotik 1 (MUS, 2nd semester: the Bayes
filter and its family) and 2 (MUS2, 3rd semester: robot vision): one lab per idea, from the histogram filter to RANSAC.
Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Sources, read-only, in `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\FH\03_FH_Master\`: `2. Semester\MUS\` (Modul 1 Basics.pdf,
MuS1_Cheatsheet.pdf; Modul 2 Gaussianfilters.pdf, EKF.pdf; Modul 3 EKFLoc.pdf; Modul 4 MCMC.pdf; Modul 0
MR_Kinematik.pdf; MUS Theoriefragen.docx, questions in words only) and `3. Semester\MUS2\` (1_Entrauschen.pdf,
2_Segmentation.pdf, 4.Edges.pdf, 10_MuS2_CNN.pdf, 11_Pose-Estimation.pdf). The drive is slow: copy a PDF to a scratch
folder first and read it with `pdftotext -enc UTF-8 -layout`; pages cited are PDF pages. Algorithm boxes and some
equations are images: render the page (`pdftoppm -r 110 -png -f N -l N`) and read it before citing. Known slips in the
sources: see README.md. Not yet used: GraphicalModels.pdf, MuS1_SS2018_PR_SLAM.pdf, and MUS2's Hough, contours,
features, classification and PCA sets (5–9). Check every equation against the slides and cite file and page. Part of
