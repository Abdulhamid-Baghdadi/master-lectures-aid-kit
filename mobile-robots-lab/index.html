Mobile and Service Robotics

After the slides of Wöber (2018–2021), Mobile- und Servicerobotik 1 (MUS) and 2 (MUS2), FH Technikum Wien, with the convolutional networks chapter by Thalhammer (2021), and the MUS 1 cheat sheet.

A learning aid: every image is a synthetic example scene and every noise comes from a seeded generator, so a lab looks the same on every visit. Pages are the PDF pages of each slide set; no text of the slides is reproduced. Where the slides contain a slip, the lab says so and uses the corrected form.
