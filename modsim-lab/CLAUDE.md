# ModSim

Interactive labs for the FH Technikum Wien master's course Modellbildung und Simulation (ModSim, 2nd semester,
lecturer A. Mario Puhwein): the exercises, not the slides, animated step by step, each step with the MATLAB that gives
its result. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`. Sources, in
`H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\FH\03_FH_Master\2. Semester\ModSim\` (read-only): `VO-Aufgaben-mit-Lösungen.pdf`
(8 worked examples, 46 pp.; its text layer garbles the maths, so read formulas from page images; transcribed with pages
in `docs/sources/vo-solutions-a.md`, `-b.md`), `Aufgabenstellung_ModSim_MKS_v01.pdf` (13 tasks, figures only;
`docs/sources/task-sheet.md`), `Zusatz Material\Bestimmung der Eigenfrequenzen des 2DoF Systems.pdf` (the TM3 note,
the two-mass values), and the group's work in `Teams - General\` (`docs/sources/student-work.md`), which sets the
MATLAB style; its slips are listed in the README. Keep the source's German symbols (k, c, k_D, c_D, φ, L₁, h_S …),
label in English with the German term once. Check MATLAB behaviour on the MathWorks reference pages before showing a
call, and cite the PDF page for every value. MATLAB is not installed: snippets are checked for undefined names, not run.
Part of `../PLAN.md`; not published.
