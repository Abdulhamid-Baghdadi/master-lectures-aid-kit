ModSim

After the worked lecture examples of Puhwein (2021a) and the task sheet of Puhwein (2021b), Modellbildung und Simulation (ModSim), FH Technikum Wien, and a TM3 note on the eigenfrequencies of a two-mass system.

A learning aid: every curve worked out the same way on every visit, every MATLAB snippet written from the view's current values. Pages are PDF pages; no text of the course is reproduced. Where the source gives a result, the lab shows it beside its own and says when they differ. The lecture gives symbols, not numbers: most values here are example values, labelled.
