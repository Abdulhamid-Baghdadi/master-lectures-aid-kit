Robot Kinematics

After the lecture slides of Ajanović (2020), Industrierobotik (IRB), FH Technikum Wien, based on slides by M. Aburaia, chapters 1–5, and the IRB 120 product specification of ABB (2020).

A learning aid: ideal rigid links and joints without play, every drawing worked out the same way on every visit. Pages are the PDF pages of each chapter's file; no text of the slides is reproduced. Where the lab and the slides differ, the lab says so.
