# Advanced Sensor Systems

Interactive labs for the FH Technikum Wien master's course Advanced Sensor Systems (ASENS, folder `ASS`, 1st semester):
one lab per idea, from echo ranging to stereo correspondence. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests:
`node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`. Sources, in `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\FH\03_FH_Master\1. Semester\ASS\`:
the slides of Kubinger (2020), `ASENS_WS2020_Folien_v1_2Seiten.pdf` (text layer; read with `pdftotext -enc UTF-8
-layout`; pages cited are PDF pages, two slides to a page, slide number beside), the MC control questions `MC2.pdf`
(Teil 1, repeated in `Fragenkatalog.pdf`) and `MC1.pdf` (Teil 2), the Intel RealSense D400 datasheet `Bericht\Manual.pdf`,
and the past exams in `Older semester\Dateien MMR17\` (questions in words only, no printed answers). Unreadable (images):
`Zusammenfassung_ASENS.pdf` and the ticks of the filled MC sheets in `Older semester\Fernlehre\`. Known slips in the
sources: p. 78 gives λmod = 15 m at 20 kHz (should be 20 MHz); the stereo table of p. 87 states 6 µm pixels but its
depths need 7.68 µm. The course is about the sensors of an autonomous car and their image processing, not about
classical transducers (PT100, strain gauges): those are in the Bachelor kit's Measurement lab. Check every equation
against the slides and cite PDF page and slide. Part of `../PLAN.md`; not published.
