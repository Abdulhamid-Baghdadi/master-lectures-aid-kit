Advanced Sensor Systems

After the slides of Kubinger (2020), Advanced Sensor Systems (ASENS), FH Technikum Wien, its multiple-choice control questions, and the Intel (2018) RealSense D400 datasheet used in its lab.

A learning aid: ideal pinhole cameras and noise-free signals, every picture worked out the same way on every visit. Pages are the PDF pages of the slides' file; no text of the slides is reproduced. The basics of measurement (bridges, strain gauges, converters) are in the Bachelor kit's Measurement lab.
